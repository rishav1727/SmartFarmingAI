APPLE DISEASE CROP MANAGEMENT GUIDE

1. Apple Scab (Venturia inaequalis)
Symptoms: Olive-green to black spots on leaves and fruit. Leaves may turn yellow and drop early. Fruit develops corky, brown lesions.
Treatment and Cure:
- Chemical Control: Apply Captan or Mancozeb fungicides during the early green tip stage. Systemic fungicides like Myclobutanil work well if the infection has already set in.
- Biological Control: Apply liquid sulfur sprays or copper octanoate organic soap. 
- Preventative Control: Rake and destroy fallen leaves in autumn to reduce the overwintering spore population. Keep the tree canopy open by pruning to allow leaves to dry rapidly.

2. Cedar Apple Rust (Gymnosporangium juniperi-virginianae)
Symptoms: Bright orange-yellow spots appear on the upper surface of leaves. Tubular structures form on the lower leaf surface, releasing rusty spores.
Treatment and Cure:
- Chemical Control: Apply Myclobutanil or Propiconazole fungicides starting at bud break and repeat every 7-14 days until flower petals fall.
- Biological Control: Spray with sulfur-based organic fungicides.
- Preventative Control: Remove nearby wild red cedar or juniper trees (which act as alternate hosts for the rust fungus) within a 1-mile radius if possible. Plant rust-resistant cultivars.

3. Apple Black Rot (Botryosphaeria obtusa)
Symptoms: Leaves show "frogeye" leaf spots (circular brown spots with purple margins). Fruit develops a firm, dark rot that turns black with concentric rings. Cankers form on branches.
Treatment and Cure:
- Chemical Control: Apply Captan or Thiophanate-methyl fungicides during the growing season.
- Biological Control: Use Bacillus subtilis or Trichoderma-based organic bio-fungicides.
- Preventative Control: Prune out dead wood and cankered branches. Remove mummified fruit from the tree and orchard floor.
